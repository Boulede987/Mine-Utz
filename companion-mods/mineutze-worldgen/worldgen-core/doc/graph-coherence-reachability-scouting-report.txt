the TRIVIAL positive case (it does not rule out a more complex sufficient condition).

only an ISOLATED row (successor NOT FOUND) would be a real, complete, constructive
counterexample. See ReachabilityScoutingChecks's own class doc for what to do next
depending on which way these land.
